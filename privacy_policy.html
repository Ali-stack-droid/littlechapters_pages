Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-24 · Effective 24 October 2026

Little Chapters is a private journal for your family's story. You use it to save photos, videos, voice notes and words about your children, and to share them with the people you invite. This policy explains what information we collect, why we need it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your memories are private by default. Only you, and the people you invite into your family space, can see them.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use your family's photos, videos, voice notes or words to train AI.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the app.
  • You can download everything you've saved, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Little Chapters is provided by Ali Shafqat, an independent developer based in Punjab, Pakistan ("Little Chapters", "we", "us"). For the purposes of data protection law, we are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

This policy applies to the Little Chapters apps for iPhone and Android and to the services that support them. It works together with our Terms and Conditions.

Information collected when you use Little Chapters

  • Sign-in and security records. When you sign in, our sign-in provider records the time, your IP address and basic information about your device and app version. We use this to keep your account secure and to stop abuse.
  • Invite code attempts. When someone enters an invite code, we record the attempt for a short time so we can block people who try to guess codes.
  • Push notification tokens. If you allow notifications, your phone gives us a token that lets us send notifications to it. We keep it with whether it's an iPhone or an Android phone and your time zone, so notifications arrive at sensible hours. The token is removed when you sign out.

Information from others

  • App Store and Google Play. If you buy a plan, Apple or Google handles the payment. Through RevenueCat, our subscription service, we receive which plan you bought, when it renews or ends, and whether it is a free trial. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • Your family. People in your family space can add memories, photos and wishes about your children, and can see your name and relationship.

What we don't collect

We don't access your contacts, we don't read your device's location, we don't use advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. We only access your camera, photo library and microphone when you choose to add a photo, video or voice note.

4. Who can see your memories

  • Just me. A memory saved as "Just me" can be seen only by you.
  • Family. A memory shared with your family can be seen by the people you've invited into your family space, limited to the children each person has been given access to.
  • Roles. Owners manage the family, its children and its invites. Contributors can add memories. Viewers can only look. Books can be opened only by the family's owners.
  • Invites. An invite code works for 7 days. The owner can remove a person at any time, and that person immediately loses access.

Little Chapters has no public profiles and no public feed. If you save or share a memory outside the app, for example to your phone's photos or a messaging app, what happens next is up to you and that service.

A small number of our staff can access stored information only when it is needed to run the service, keep it secure or answer your request for help, and they are bound by confidentiality.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who run Little Chapters for us, under contracts that let them use your information only to provide their service to us:
    • Supabase: our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions.
    • RevenueCat: managing subscriptions bought through the App Store or Google Play.
    • Apple and Google: signing in with your Apple or Google account, payments, and app distribution.
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications to your phone. It receives your phone's push token and the notification text, such as a family member's name and a short caption, but not your photos, videos or voice notes.
    • Our email delivery service: sending sign-in and password reset emails.
  • The people you invite, as described in section 4.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or when it is needed to protect someone's safety, to prevent fraud or to defend our legal rights. We'll push back on requests that aren't valid.
  • A new owner, if Little Chapters is sold or merged. Your information would stay protected by this policy, and we'd tell you before it became subject to a different one.

6. Children's information

Little Chapters is made for parents, guardians and their families. Accounts are for adults: you must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live, to create one. Children don't have accounts and don't use Little Chapters themselves.

When you add a child, you confirm that you are their parent or legal guardian, or that you have their parent's or guardian's permission. We use a child's information only to keep their family's journal. If you believe a child has created an account, or that a child's information was added without permission, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is open, so your family's story is there when you come back. When you delete something, we delete it:

  • A memory: the memory and its photos, videos and voice notes are deleted from our servers.
  • A child: the child, their memories and wishes are deleted.
  • Leaving a family, or being removed from one: you lose access straight away. The memories you added stay with that family. If you'd rather they didn't, delete them before you leave; deleting your account also deletes them.
  • Your account: your account and profile are deleted right away. Every family you own is deleted too, with all of its children, memories, photos, videos and books, including what other people added there. In families you joined, the memories you added are deleted and everything else stays with the family. The app shows you exactly what will be deleted before you confirm.

Deleted files are removed from storage straight away, or shortly after if a removal has to be retried. Copies in our provider's backups are overwritten within 30 days. Expired invite code attempts are cleared after a day. We keep subscription records as long as tax and accounting law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

In the app

  • Download your archive: Profile → Download your archive saves the original photos, videos and voice notes, every caption, date and tag, and your children's details.
  • Correct your details: change your name, photo, children's details and memories at any time.
  • Choose who sees what: pick "Just me" or "Family" for each memory, and remove people from your family.
  • Notifications: turn each kind on or off in Profile → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account.

Your legal rights

  • access the personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it;
  • withdraw consent you've given, without affecting what happened before;
  • not be treated differently for using these rights.

To use any of these rights, email alicheema12455@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm it's really you before we act. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK, though we'd like the chance to help first.

We don't sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined in California law.

9. How we protect it

  • Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Our provider stores the database and files encrypted at rest.
  • Photos, videos and voice notes are kept in private storage and are opened through links that expire after a short time.
  • Who can see what is checked on our servers for every request, not just in the app.
  • Passwords are stored hashed, and changing your password signs you out on your other devices.

Little Chapters is not end-to-end encrypted, which is what allows you to share memories with family and to download your archive. No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password that you don't use elsewhere, and sign out on shared devices. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. On your phone

Little Chapters keeps some information on your phone so it works quickly and offline: your session, settings, recent memories and image previews, and memories you saved while offline that are still waiting to upload. Memories that haven't finished uploading exist only on that phone, so they are lost if you delete the app or sign out before they upload.

Memory reminders and monthly age moments are scheduled by the app on your phone. Other notifications, such as family updates and birthday wishes, are sent from our servers through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Signing out removes your family's information from Little Chapters on that phone.

11. Where it is stored

Your information is stored on servers in Japan (Tokyo). Some of our service providers may process it in other countries, including the United States. When information leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on approved safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum.
