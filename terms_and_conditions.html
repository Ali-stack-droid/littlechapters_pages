3. Families, roles and invites

Memories live in a family space. Each person in it has one of three roles:

RoleWhat they can do
OwnerManages the family: adds and edits children, invites and removes people, chooses the family's plan, and makes books.
ContributorAdds memories about the children they've been given access to, and sees the memories shared with the family.
ViewerSees the memories shared with the family about the children they've been given access to.
  • Owners are responsible for their family space. Only invite people you trust, and give each person access only to the children they should see.
  • Invites expire. An invite code works for 7 days. Anyone with the code can use it in that time, so share it only with the person it is meant for.
  • People can leave or be removed. An owner can remove someone at any time. When someone leaves or is removed, they lose access straight away and the memories they added stay with the family. Deleting an account deletes the memories that person added in every family.
  • If an owner deletes their account, every family they own is deleted, with all of its children, memories, photos, videos and books, including what other people added. Before an owner confirms, the app shows how many people and memories will be affected. If you add memories to someone else's family, keep your own copies of anything you can't bear to lose.

A plan belongs to the person who buys it and applies to the families they own. People who join your family use your family's plan.

7. Books

Books turn your memories into pages with themes and covers. Books are available to the owners of a family. On a paid plan, owners can read, write and add pages to every book. On the Free plan, owners can view and edit only the first page of each book. The memories inside books are never deleted when a plan ends.

Book themes are inspired designs created for Little Chapters. They aren't connected with, or endorsed by, any author, publisher or film studio.
